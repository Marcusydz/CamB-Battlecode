## 2. Death trace (`DEATH_TRACE`, v272 = v1.5.0 + trace, behaviour unchanged)

Each champion keeps its last 8 turns (position, distance home, health, act, allies near, up to 3
enemies with health and distance) and prints them after the death line. `scripts/traces.py` reads them.

Local, v1.5.0 vs v1.2.0, 80 games, 835 of our deaths:
- 694 happened while retreating (`lowhp`/`lowhp-fight`).
- The retreat began at 20% health on average; 240 lasted a single turn, 89 lasted 8+ turns.
- 396 retreats gained no distance toward home: the step maximises distance from any threat up to 6
  tiles before it looks at home distance, so champions with enemies on two sides dither in place.
- 354 had no enemy champion in sight: camps. Monoliths hit for 200 a turn at range 5, so a champion
  that starts retreating at 30% (360 of 1200) dies inside the range.

Pre-existing: `engine error 97: Unit is not in vision` / `98: No visible entity...` abort ~13 champion
turns per 80 games (v1.5.0 too). Not yet traced.

## 5. Contest blowout trace (v160_base, 0–1000, kills 3–57)

The enemy (Ryth/Vesp/Tavi/Prism/Marc) camped 4–8 tiles outside our spawn. Our champions alternated
`approach` and `back-off` every turn (rounds 90–93, 809–816): an enemy at 4 tiles triggers a back-off,
next turn it is at 5, so the champion walks back toward the same camp and into spell range again.
Vesper Vale died channelling a recall started with enemies at 7 tiles that then closed in.
Idea `BACKOFF_AVOID=N`: after a back-off/disengage/outnumbered retreat, avoid the camp we were heading
for (if within 8 tiles) for N rounds, so the next plan picks another camp.

## 7. Engine errors (97/98) found

The errors came from `cambc::vision::champions(team::ally)` itself raising when an ally unit is at the
edge of vision. Every throw aborted the rest of that champion's turn (the turn loop catches and yields).
- My trace recording added most of them (54 per 80 games vs 13 before); now guarded.
- `visible_allies()` wrapper (like `visible_enemies()`), used for all 13 ally queries: the remaining
  in-turn errors drop to 3 per 80 games.
- End-of-turn steps (ward, distance fields, trace) are guarded separately and logged with `ERROR_LOG`.
