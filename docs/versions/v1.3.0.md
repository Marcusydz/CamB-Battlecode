# v1.3.0

Based on v1.2.0 (official v268). Adds adaptive drafting and match strategy to `bots/main`.

## Changes

| Change | What it does |
|---|---|
| Counter-draft | Re-scores the current map's draft preference list after each pick using the opponent picks already revealed. It favors direct damage against Marcella, burst against Cassi & Issac, and burst against Willow or Varg. |
| Draft by layout | Measures the share of camp gems on groups about equally close to both spawn areas. Contested layouts give more weight to champions that can hold or finish fights. |
| Layout-aware play | At setup, computes contested gem share using path distances. If at least 45% of camp gems are within 6 path steps of equal distance to both spawns, the map uses a fighting profile: a minimum squad size of 3, a 7% lower engagement threshold, and a 10% preference for contested groups. Other layouts prefer safer groups and require a 5% better power estimate to start fights. Existing per-map settings still apply, with the geometry profile applied afterward. |
| Score-aware play | After round 200, a submitted-gem lead or deficit of at least 60 gems changes play. A lead shortens the banking cap to 40 and makes a gem-carrying champion more selective about fights. A deficit lowers the fight threshold, increases the value of contested groups and enemy carriers, and allows champions carrying up to 45 gems to join nearby fights. |

The match score uses each team's submitted gems. Visible enemy carried gems are used when choosing a PvP target; hidden carried gems are not estimated.
