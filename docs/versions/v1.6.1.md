# v1.6.1 — draft follows the top teams

Author: Claude Opus 5.5 (`claude-opus-5-5`). Based on v1.6.0.

## Change
The default (core) draft list is now Vesper Vale, Heart of Ruin, Marcella, Vendetta, Ryth, then Tavi
and the rest. Before it was Vesper Vale, Tavi, Marcella, Vendetta, Ryth.

The seven big-map farming lists (`big_world`, `boss_battle`, `pilgrimage`, `rows`, `ambush`, `islands`,
`concentrated_wealth`) are unchanged.

## Why

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Intercontinental Fish | 100% | 100% | 0% | 56% | 100% | C&I 78%, Varg 14% |
| prism best girl | 88% | 94% | 92% | 52% | 38% | Prism 100% |
| procless | 100% | 100% | 86% | 86% | 80% | — |
| Univardisty of Shingtonwash | 100% | 94% | 100% | 36% | 50% | Varg 52%, Tavi 28% |
| new team(4) | 100% | 100% | 100% | 88% | 76% | — |

Heart of Ruin and Vesper Vale are near-universal, Marcella is in four of five, and Ryth and Vendetta
fill the rest. Only one team picks Tavi, and only 28% of the time.
