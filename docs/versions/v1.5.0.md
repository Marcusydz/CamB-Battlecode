# v1.5.0

Based on v1.4.0. Author: Claude Opus 5.5 (`claude-opus-5-5`), with the analysis in
`docs/v1.5.0_findings.md`.

Replaces the draft with a fixed, data-backed lineup and fixes how low-health champions escape.
The live bot before this was v268 (v1.2.0 code: identified by matching ladder drafts to local runs).

## Why

Against teams rated 1680–1800, v268 lost 40 of 40 unranked games on our weakest maps. Per game we
had 4 kills and 30 deaths against their 25 kills and 9 deaths, and 2.2k earned gold against 9.1k.
Their duelists (Vesper Vale, Vendetta, Heart of Ruin, Ren, Marcella) caught our Stellar, Varg and
Willow alone or in pairs, and the kill gold snowballed.

On 41k sampled ranked games of the whole ladder, a logistic model with each team's rating as an offset
gives the same picture. Vesper Vale, Tavi, Marcella, Vendetta and Ryth win more than their team's
rating predicts; Stellar, Ren and Cassi & Issac win less. v268 drafted Stellar in nearly every game.

## Changes

| Change | What it does |
|---|---|
| Draft | Picks down one list: Vesper Vale, Tavi, Marcella, Vendetta, Ryth, then the rest. 3-pick maps get the first three, 4-pick maps the first four. Seven big open maps keep v1.2.0's farming lineups (big_world, boss_battle: Heart of Ruin/Varg/Stellar/Ryth/Willow; pilgrimage, rows, ambush: Vesper Vale/Varg/Stellar/Ryth/Willow; islands, concentrated_wealth: Vesper Vale/Varg/Vendetta/Ryth/Willow). |
| Full lists | Every list holds all 12 champions. v1.4.0's lists had 11 slots, so each one left out a champion (list 16 had no Vesper Vale, list 17 no Heart of Ruin). |
| Map recognition | Maps are matched on width, height, wall count and camp-group count. Packs and gold_rush (31x31, no walls) and elkring and gemfight (30x30, no walls) were indistinguishable before. |
| `SEEN_EVERY_TURN` (bug fix) | Enemy positions are remembered every turn. Before, they were only updated in the PvP step, which a champion below the retreat threshold never reaches. It fled from where enemies had been, and once those sightings were 3 rounds old it started a recall next to its chaser. |
| `LOWHP_FIGHT_KILL_ONLY` | A champion below the retreat threshold that is being hit fights back only if it can kill an enemy in range. Otherwise it keeps retreating. |
| `LOW_HP` 0.4 → 0.3, `THREAT_RANGE` 5 → 6 | Retreat at 30% health. Do not start a recall with an enemy within 6 tiles. |
| `DEATH_LOG` | One line per death on stdout: round, champion, position, last action, carried gems, enemies seen near, allies near. Ladder replays include our stdout, so `scripts/deaths.py` can read the logs from ladder games. |
| Golem | `camp_stats`/`camp_range` handle `camp_type::golem` by name. The values are unchanged. |

The model-based lineup search, tried first, did worse than the fixed list (39–42 of 80 against
v1.2.0 vs 51 of 80). Its many small synergy and counter terms outweighed the champion values.

## Results

### Local (cambc 3.0.5, all 40 maps, both sides)

Three seeded runs (unseeded and seeds 1–2), 240 games per opponent:

| Opponent | Wins | Mean gem margin |
|---|---|---|
| v1.2.0 (live v268) | **184 / 240 (77%)** | **+280** |
| v1.4.0 | **189 / 240 (79%)** | **+371** |

0 bot faults. Every map has a non-negative mean margin except broken_curve (4/12, −24).

How it built up (paired runs, unseeded + seed 1, against both opponents, 320 games):

| Step | Wins / 320 | Margin |
|---|---|---|
| v1.4.0 play + new draft | 220 | +199 |
| + `SEEN_EVERY_TURN` | 231 | +244 |
| + `LOWHP_FIGHT_KILL_ONLY` | 239 | +320 |
| + `LOW_HP` 0.3, `THREAT_RANGE` 6 | 255 | +334 |

The last step was confirmed on fresh seeds 3–4: 254/320 (+325) vs 238/320 (+300) without it.
These are local games against our own older bots, which play nothing like the gold-tier teams.

## Still weak

These teams are still ahead of us. With accurate enemy positions, the death log shows 18.5 of
26 deaths a game (worst-maps set) happen while retreating below the health threshold: escapes fail
against duelists with dashes. 8.6 deaths a game are alone, 12.8 with allies nearby, and 4.8 have no
enemy in sight (mostly camps). Next ideas are in `TODO.md`.

## Tools added

- `scripts/pull_matches.py`: our matches to `results/matches.jsonl`. `scripts/ladder_stats.py`: win rates by map, pick, opponent and rating.
- `scripts/pull_all.py`, `scripts/sample_ladder.sh`: sample the whole ranked ladder by hour (crafted time cursors).
- `scripts/draft_model.py`: the draft model (rating offset, champion value, per-map delta, synergy, counters).
- `scripts/groups.py`: report an unranked batch and download its summaries and replays. `scripts/fetch_summaries.py`, `scripts/summary_stats.py`: per-game metrics.
- `scripts/deaths.py`: aggregate `DEATH_LOG` lines. `scripts/per_map.py`: per-map record from arena output.
- `scripts/exp.sh`, `scripts/exp_fresh.sh`: paired switch experiments against v1.2.0 and v1.4.0, and confirmation on fresh seeds.
- `bots/fingerprint`: prints each map's fingerprint (`results/map_fingerprints.txt`).
