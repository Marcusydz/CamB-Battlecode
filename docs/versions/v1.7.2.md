# v1.7.2 — carrier-aware retreat experiment

Author: Copilot (AI assistant using Copilot SDK in VS Code), 23 October 2026.

Based on v1.7.1/v277, which is based on v1.7.0/v275, v1.6.1/v274, and the
official v1.6.0/v273 runtime fixes.

## Analysis of the v1.7.1 local regression

The v1.7.1 threshold raised champion-threat retreats from 30% to 35% whenever
an enemy was within five tiles. In the local all-map comparison against the
exact v1.7.0 source, 183 of 260 observed deaths were retreat deaths. Example
traces showed champions carrying zero gems and standing with two or three
allies still abandoning group fights. v1.7.1 scored 34/80 locally with a
mean margin of -65.

## Change

The 35% threshold now applies only when:

- the champion is carrying gems, or
- the champion has no nearby ally within five tiles.

A champion with no gems and nearby allies retains the normal 30% threshold so
it can stay in a potentially winning group fight.

## Validation

The candidate was submitted as server version **278** and tested against:

| Opponent | Rating | Record |
|---|---:|---:|
| al | 1703 | 2/5 |
| 67 Lions | 1754 | 2/5 |
| team | 1784 | 1/5 |
| **Total** | **1703–1784** | **5/15 (33.3%)** |

Per-game averages were 582 submitted gems, 667 collected gems, 7 kills, and
17 deaths. This is worse than v1.7.1's 9/15 in its test batch.

The local candidate comparison completed 78/80 games with 0 bot faults; two
ambush runs produced no statistics from the local runner. The completed local
result was 34/78 with a mean margin of -69, so this change is not locally
stronger either.

## Release status

This is an experimental patch release retained for reproducibility and further
testing. **v1.7.1 remains the stronger candidate** based on current unranked
evidence. The 1700–1800 ELO goal is not yet demonstrated, and v1.8.0 should
wait for a better-tested strategic change rather than another retreat-threshold
tweak.
