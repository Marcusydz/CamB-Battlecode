# v1.6.0

Based on v1.5.0. Author: Claude Opus 5.5 (`claude-opus-5-5`). Working notes: `docs/v1.6.0_findings.md`.
Submitted as official v273. Goal: beat teams rated 1700–1800.

## Changes

| Change | What it does |
|---|---|
| `visible_allies()` (bug fix) | `vision::champions(team::ally)` can raise (engine errors 97/98) when an ally is at the edge of vision; each throw aborted that champion's whole turn. All 13 ally queries now go through a wrapper that returns an empty list instead. |
| Guarded end-of-turn steps | Ward placement, distance-field precompute and the trace each catch their own errors (`ERROR_LOG` names the step). |
| `ENDGAME_THEIRS=900` | Once the enemy has banked 900 gems, bank everything we carry. v272 lost five close games vs 1700+ teams holding unbanked gems. |
| `DEATH_TRACE` | After each death line, prints our last 8 turns (position, distance home, health, act, allies, up to 3 enemies with health and distance). `scripts/traces.py` analyses them from local or ladder replays. |
| Off by default (tested, not adopted) | `DANGER_RETREAT` (1, or 2 = monoliths only), `RETREAT_SAFE`, `BACKOFF_AVOID`, `LOW_HP_ENEMY`, `TAVI_STRENGTH`. |

## Results

- Unranked vs eight 1703–1763 teams, all 40 maps once, same opponents/maps/sides: v272 (v1.5.0 + trace) **15/40**, v273 (v1.6.0) **15/40**. Fights are now even on average (kills 11 vs 11, deaths 14 vs 13 per game); the gap is farming speed and a few blowouts.
- Local paired tests (320 games vs v1.2.0 and v1.4.0): the fixes are neutral to slightly positive; no retreat or farming tweak survived confirmation on fresh seeds (see findings §4, §8).
- Engine errors in local games: 54 per 80 → 3 per 80.

v1.6.0 does **not** yet reach the goal of beating 1700–1800 teams; it is a cleanup/diagnostics release on top of v1.5.0.

## What the traces showed

- ~80% of our deaths happen while retreating, begun at ~20% health; about half the retreats gain no distance toward home (the step maximises distance from threats before home distance, so champions dither).
- Against the ladder the nearest enemy during those retreats is usually 2–4 tiles away (chasers); locally it is camps.
- **Monoliths** (200 a turn, range 5) kill our champions even when they retreat at full health. On contest, where the gems are in monoliths, we died 86 times in one game against a passive bot and never reach 1000 gems.
- Ally-and-enemy back-off loops: a champion alternates `approach` / `back-off` every turn at the edge of enemy spell range.

## Next steps (recommended)

1. **Monolith strategy** (contest, starvation, monolith-heavy maps): tank rotation — the nearest champion takes the hits, so swap the front champion before it drops below 5 × 200 HP, keep the rest at range 5+, and only start once the team (and items) can deal ~20,000 damage before rotating out. Check first whether monoliths regenerate health between attempts.
2. **Retreat pathing**: pick the step that gets closer to home while staying out of the chaser's attack range, retreat toward allies when they are nearer than spawn, and recall from behind walls/bushes. Test it on the ladder (unranked A/B with `scripts/replicate_batch.py`), not locally: our own bots don't chase, so every local retreat test came out neutral.
3. **Farming speed**: 1700+ teams collect ~15% more gems and deal ~15% more monster damage. Camp selection and walking (33% of turns vs a passive bot) are the next place to look; `scripts/farmspeed.py` measures it without an opponent.
4. **Caster/stun lineups** (Ryth/Stel/Tavi/Prism/C&I) blew us out on contest, elk_arena, starfish, stampede. A local sparring copy drafting that lineup lost 59/80 to us overall but beat us on gem_fields, collision, corridors and expanse — candidates for per-map draft overrides.
5. Keep using `scripts/unranked_high_elo.py` (MIN_ELO/MAX_ELO/MAX_GAMES) and `scripts/replicate_batch.py` for paired ladder checks; 40 games per arm is about ±3 wins of noise.
