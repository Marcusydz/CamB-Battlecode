## 7. Draft model on global ladder games (`scripts/draft_model.py`)

Sampled ~1,200 ranked games per hour of the whole ladder since engine 1.0.2 (`scripts/sample_ladder.sh`).
Logistic model: Elo offset (current ratings) + champion value + per-map delta + pair synergy + counters.
- Teams ≥1550 only (3.6k games): draft adds nothing over Elo on held-out games. Team play dominates.
- Teams ≥1300 (15k games): held-out log-loss 0.6837 vs 0.6874 for Elo alone. Global values (logit):
  Vesper +0.17, Vendetta +0.16, Marcella +0.13, Ryth +0.11, HoR +0.09, Tavi +0.05, Prism −0.01,
  Varg −0.02, Willow −0.02, Ren −0.16, Stellar −0.18, C&I −0.20.
- Our usual lineup HoR/Varg/Stel/Ryth/Will sums to about −0.02; Vesper/Vendetta/Marcella/Ryth/HoR
  to about +0.66 (≈ +16 points of win probability at even Elo).

## 13. Unranked results of v270 and the low-health fixes

- Batch 2 (batch 1's opponents/maps/sides): **5/40** (was 0/40). Kills 4 → 7, champion damage 16.9k → 26.5k,
  submitted gems 256 → 376 per game. Deaths still 29 per game.
- Batch 3 (eight other 1700–1800 teams, all 40 maps once): **10/40**; deaths 18 vs 7, kills 5 vs 15.
- Death log (our stdout in the replays, `scripts/deaths.py`): of 29.5 deaths a game, 12.2 came in
  `lowhp-fight` and 10.7 in `lowhp` — already below the retreat threshold. Tavi 7.9, Vesper 7.1,
  Marcella 5.7, Vendetta 5.6, Ryth 3.2 deaths a game.
- **Bug:** enemy memory (`seen`) was refreshed only in `pvp()`/`outnumbered()`, which run after the
  low-health branch returns. A retreating champion planned its path from stale enemy positions and,
  once they were 3 rounds old, recalled next to its chaser. `SEEN_EVERY_TURN=1` refreshes it every turn:
  231/320, +244 (baseline 220/320, +199). (Also made the death log's enemy count wrong: "no enemy" ≈ stale.)
- `LOWHP_FIGHT_KILL_ONLY=1`: below LOW_HP and being hit, fight back only if a kill is in reach, else keep
  retreating: **239/320, +320** on top of the fix.
