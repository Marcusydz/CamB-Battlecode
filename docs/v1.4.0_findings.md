## 2. Key Tactical & Draft Findings

### A. The Power of the "Big Three" Core in 1.6k–1.8k Teams
1. **Heart of Ruin (`CT::heart_of_ruin`)**:
   - Ranked #1 opener across high-performing opponents (Chain User, Rogue Fog Walkers, cheesynachos, umamusume, noobers).
   - Massive survivability + AoE 1.5x stun dash (`HOR_DASH`) secures first camp clears and wins early skirmishes.
2. **Vesper Vale (`CT::vesper_vale`)**:
   - Passive: +33% gold bonus, high base strength (55), 3-tile range, attack reset + dash mobility. Rapidly snowballs gold into stats.
3. **Marcella (`CT::marcella`) / Varg (`CT::varg`)**:
   - Marcella is immune to stuns (completely invalidating enemy Prism/Tavi/Ryth/Ren stuns) and gains +2 gold per tributed gem.
   - Varg has splash basic attacks + 2.5x focus shield. High clear speed on multi-monster camps.

### B. Critical Architectural Flaw in v1.4.0 Draft System
1. **The 11-Champion Array Truncation Bug**:
   - Total champion pool is 12 champions (Ryth, Vesper Vale, Vendetta, Cassi & Issac, Stellar, Prism, Marcella, Willow, Ren, Varg, Tavi, Heart of Ruin).
   - `using List = std::array<D, 11>;` in `draft.cpp` truncated each list to 11 champions, omitting one champion completely!
   - In List 16 (`POST_PATCH_LIST`), **Vesper Vale was omitted completely**.
   - In List 17 (`VESPER`), **Heart of Ruin was omitted completely**.
   - In List 20 (`PRISM_MAPS_LIST`), **Vesper Vale was omitted completely**.
   - Consequently, our bot **never had the capability to draft both Heart of Ruin and Vesper Vale together** in any match! Meanwhile, top 1.6k–1.8k opponents (Chain User, Rogue Fog Walkers, cheesynachos, umamusume) run Heart of Ruin + Vesper Vale + Marcella/Varg as their core frontline/backline engine.

2. **Prism First Pick Toxicity**:
   - `01gold_rush`, `00collision`: Bot drafted Prism first due to `PRISM_MAPS_LIST = 20`, losing both games by large margins.
   - Ladder data confirmed Prism first is 8–29 (22% winrate). Prism is fragile (700 HP) and easily collapsed on.

3. **Marcella Missing/Buried**:
   - Marcella has 1200 HP, 100 HP growth, +2 gold/gem tributed, stun immunity, and a stun on attack reset.
   - In List 16 and 17, Marcella was relegated to Rank 7, behind Willow and Cassi & Issac.
   - In `traits()`, Marcella was missing `control` and `economy`, and falsely given `sustain`.
   - Enemy control compositions were never countered with Marcella.

4. **Artificial Willow Bias**:
   - `if (pick == D::willow && front_count > 0) s.synergy += 0.4;` artificially boosted fragile Willow (700 HP) into virtually every 5-champion lineup.
   - Willow's heal requires an ally within 4 tiles on an 8-turn cooldown, which is rarely utilized in spread camp farming.

5. **Cassi & Issac Fragility on Small/Contested Maps**:
   - On `01broken_curve` (3 picks) and `00interlock` (4 picks), drafting Cassi & Issac yielded instant losses. When one dies, both die, giving 100g + all gems to the opponent.

### C. In-Game Strategy Deficiencies
1. **Camp Contesting & Denial**:
   - In `TODO.md` and `docs/UnusedRules.md`, Rule 9 (resetting enemy capture progress) had `CONTEST` tested but left off in v1.2.0.
   - In 1.6k-1.8k matches, opponents contest or capitalize when we retreat prematurely.
2. **Altar Tributing and Banking Speed**:
   - Tributing takes `ceil(gems / 10)` turns. Walking into altars without clearing nearby threats leads to deaths while channeling or right before channeling.
3. **Prism Banking Cap**:
   - Prism carries too many gems trying to scale focus (+1.5 focus/gem) and gets killed, losing all gems.
